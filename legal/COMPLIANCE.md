## 1. University & Regulatory Compliance
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act**: Compliance with data minimization, purpose limitation, and student right to erasure.
- **Educational Fair Use**: All academic materials, lecture slides, and AI workshop repositories comply with educational fair use standards.
- **Ethical AI Code of Conduct**: As the RAISE AI Club (Responsible Artificial Intelligence), all student software hosted or linked through the platform must adhere to ethical AI guidelines regarding bias, transparency, and safety.
