# Privacy Policy (PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-08-18
Organization: AI Foundry (RAISE AI CLUB) - Dayananda Sagar University

---

## 1. Information Collected
AI Foundry collects only necessary information provided voluntarily by students and visitors:
- **Event Registrations**: Name, University/College, Official Student Email Address, Phone Number, Branch/Semester.
- **Member Recruitment**: Name, Contact Details, Portfolio/GitHub Links, Technical Skills, Statement of Interest.
- **Inquiries**: Name, Email Address, Message Contents.

---

## 2. Use of Information
Collected data is used strictly for:
- Coordinating event logistics, badge printing, and hackathon communications.
- Evaluating applications for club team membership.
- Responding to student and faculty inquiries.

---

## 3. Data Protection & Non-Commercialization
- We **never** sell, rent, or trade student personal information to third parties.
- Data is stored securely in encrypted cloud storage or restricted local databases.
- Students may request deletion of their registration records at any time by contacting `aifoundry@dsu.edu.in`.
